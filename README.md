## 🔬 Current Research

### When is it worth going quantum?

My PhD research investigates whether characteristics of datasets and
learning problems can predict, **a priori**, when variational quantum
models may outperform classical machine learning approaches.

Research exploring quantum computational approaches to strongly
interacting systems and dense nuclear matter.
